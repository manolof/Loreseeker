## Description (paste into the description editor)

**Not the fastest way to 60. The most complete one.**

Loreseeker is a quest guide for **WoW Forever** for players who want to see the world and its stories, not race to max level, without giving up the streamlined "what's next?" feel of a leveling guide.

It isn't a hand-written route. Loreseeker reads the full quest database through Questie, works out what *your* character can do right now, and plans a trip around it: picking up, doing and handing in quests in a sensible order, using the flight paths you have, boats and the tram.

### Features

- **Do (almost) everything:** every quest your character can do, grey ones included. Too-hard quests wait in a *Later* queue until you level up. You choose how far above your level counts as too hard.
- **Plans your trip:** up to 16 steps ahead, grouped into stops, so everything to pick up, do and hand in at one place happens together.
- **Real travel:** walking, the flight paths you've discovered, boats, zeppelins and the Deeprun Tram. When flying is faster, the arrow points at the flight master.
- **Lore first:** about 100 hand-picked storylines (the Defias, the Onyxia attunements, Tirion Fordring, Linken and many more), grouped by zone with a short description each, and given priority in the route. Star any other chain you like. Click any quest to see its whole storyline.
- **Lore journal:** saves the text of every quest you read, so you can reread the story later.
- **Listen:** plays Spoken's voice-over when it's installed, and otherwise reads the quest with the game's text-to-speech.
- **PvP-server friendly:** skip a quest, a storyline, the area around you or a whole zone, each with a note and an optional "remind me at level 40". A one-click preset covers the contested zones.
- **Smart items:** compares drop rates, nearby vendors, the Auction House and your own recipes, and suggests the cheapest way.
- **Copes with gaps in the data:** it spots breadcrumbs that are no longer offered, follow-ups missing their prerequisite, and NPCs that don't offer what the database says.
- **Looks like the game:** stock Blizzard frames, fonts and icons, no custom skins.

### Languages
English, German, French, Spanish, Russian and Simplified Chinese. Quest, NPC and zone names are always in your client's language.

### Requirements
- **Questie** and **QuestieDB** (required)
- **Spoken** (optional, for voice-over)

### Commands
`/lore` opens the window. `/lore plan` prints your trip, `/lore travel` shows your known flight points, `/lore quest <id>` explains a quest's status, and `/lore bug <text>` saves a bug report.

### Privacy
Everything stays on your computer: Loreseeker never sends anything anywhere.

### Bug reports and ideas
Please report on **GitHub Issues: https://github.com/manolof/Loreseeker/issues** (the form tells you exactly what to include).
In game, type `/lore bug what happened`, then `/reload`, and attach the `Loreseeker.lua` file from `WTF/Account/<account>/SavedVariables`.
